## Research

**MEOWN: a physics-informed neural framework for rapid prediction of muon stopping sites in crystalline materials**
First author. Under review at Physical Review B. Quantum Materials Lab, RGIPT.

**Homogeneous to Multi-Scale Inhomogeneous Complex Magnetism in Ba<sub>3</sub>LnRu<sub>2</sub>O<sub>9</sub> (Ln = Ho, Gd), Probed by µSR**
Contributing author. Under review at Physical Review B.
S. Ghosh, E. Kushwaha, M. Kumar, G. Roy, K. Sharma, A. Pandey, F. L. Pratt, S. Cottrell, D. T. Adroja, T. Basu.

**Grokking in Root-Function Networks**
Ongoing. Studies grokking in networks trained on x<sup>1/k</sup> for k = 2 to 10, with the hypothesis of a critical threshold k* above which generalization permanently fails. x<sup>1/4</sup> grokked after a delay of about 5,400 steps; x<sup>1/5</sup> did not generalize within 30,000 steps. An arXiv preprint is in preparation.
