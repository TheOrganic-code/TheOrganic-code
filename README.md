i'm ayush pandey, an undergraduate researcher at the quantum materials lab, [rgipt](https://www.rgipt.ac.in/). i work on physics-informed machine learning, muon spin spectroscopy (µSR) and the places where data-driven models have to respect the physics underneath them. i'm pursuing a b.tech in mathematics and computing (2025-2029).

my main project is [meown](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17063), a physics-informed framework that predicts where muons stop in crystals, which is one of the key unknowns when interpreting µSR experiments. it combines a polarizable electrostatic model, a 3d cnn and symmetry-aware optimization to go from crystal structure to ranked stopping sites and dipolar fields in minutes on a normal computer. i'm the first author, and it is under review at physical review b.

in the lab i also work with inelastic neutron scattering, raman and xrd, and i was awarded neutron beam time at the spallation neutron source, oak ridge national laboratory, as on-site pi (ipts-36564). at qinetic research labs i look at quantum error correction and quantum machine learning. previously i was an ai engineer intern at digitwin technology, fine-tuning language models with lora/qlora and building rag pipelines.

i also build tools for earth observation. satfetch is a cross-modal search engine for indian satellite data (optical, sar, multispectral and text), and it was selected for the next round of isro bharatiya antariksh hackathon 2026 (ps-11, india rank #2). embersentinel is a physics-informed convlstm for heat-wave risk across the indo-gangetic plains, with a poster accepted at icorsi 2026.

other things i've made: a [probabilistic-bit simulator](https://github.com/TheOrganic-code/P-bit-Simulator) for ising systems and boltzmann machines, a neural network framework from scratch in rust (neurarust), and a study of grokking in networks trained on root functions, which is still in preparation.

i also contributed to a paper on complex magnetism in ba₃lnru₂o₉ (ln = ho, gd) probed by µSR, under review at physical review b, and a co-authored paper accepted at estic. i qualified jee advanced 2025, was a finalist at the union bank ideathon, and serve as training and placement coordinator for the department of mathematical sciences at rgipt.

i mostly write python, c++ and rust, with pytorch, numpy, scipy, pymatgen and faiss doing most of the work. right now i'm learning gpu programming with triton and cuda, and more quantum computing.
